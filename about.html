About Craig Adams

Craig Adams, hailing from Dunedin-New Zealand, began his musical journey 15 years ago on the awards circuit, quickly making his mark by winning every competition across New Zealand. A three-time male finalist at the NZ Country Music Awards, he also earned the prestigious title of Male Vocalist of the Year at the Texas Sounds International Country Music Awards. As a-singer-songwriter, Craig effortlessly delivers everything from silky ballads to high-energy country rock, whether performing beloved covers or captivating originals. He has toured through New Zealand with two album releases, headlining Australian festivals such as the Barham Country Stampede and the Terara Country Music Festival. Craig has also had the honour of touring as an opening guest for New Zealand’s incredible Suzanne Prentice (OBE). Renowned for his magnetic stage presence, he has an ability to engage audiences of all ages, holding their attention in the palm of his hand. With every performance, Craig creates a genuine connection that leaves crowds inspired and wanting more. A must-see talent in the country music scene, Craig Adams continues to shine as one of New Zealand’s most dynamic and engaging performers. Craig is down to earth, humble and a genuine Kiwi bloke, a born entertainer with X-factor talent leaving audiences hungry for more after each show.
