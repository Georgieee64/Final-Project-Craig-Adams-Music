Country High

Craig Adam's country album

Country High is a country music album by Craig Adams, released in 2013. Featuring 12 tracks, 7 of which being originals, these being "Count On Me", "Please Come Home Tonight", "Run", "Take Me Home With You", "Country High" and "A Woman's Eyes" the album has a total running time of 46 minutes and 19 seconds. It celebrates country living, love, relationships, and the simple pleasures of life. With songs that reflect a down-to-earth country spirit, heartfelt emotions, and stories of everyday experiences, Craig Adams brings a collection for fans who appreciate traditional country music and memorable melodies. Highlights include We’re From The Country,”“ “Country High,” “Take Me Home With You,” and “Please Come Home Tonight.”
